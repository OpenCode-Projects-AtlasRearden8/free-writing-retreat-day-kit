Retreat-Day Schedule

Sprints, breaks, snacks. The decisions are already made — just follow the clock. (Times shift freely; the rhythm is what matters.)

Sprint rules

  1. The timer decides; you don't.
  2. When it rings, stop — even mid-sentence. Stopping on purpose makes the next sprint easier.
  3. No editing today. Editing gets its own day.
  4. Every break includes water. Non-negotiable.
  5. If a sprint is going badly, shorten it. A bad 10 minutes is fine; a bad 50 is punishment.

Scaling options

  • Half-day: run blocks from 9:00 through lunch only (about 4 sprints).
  • Low-energy day: 15-minute sprints, 20-minute breaks, skip blocks 6–8.
  • With a friend: all blocks are the same; you just have someone to wave at across the table.
