Invite-One-Friend Planner

One friend. Not five, not a whole Discord. One. This sheet turns “maybe I should invite someone” into a sent message within ten minutes.

Step 1 · Pick the person (2 minutes)

Who would make the day easier, not more exciting?

  • Good pick: someone who also wants quiet time to make something — the friend you can sit beside in two hours of silence without it being weird.
  • Skip for now: anyone whose messages you'd want to answer mid-retreat, anyone you'd want to impress, anyone who might ask to see the work.

My pick: ____________________   (a first name — or “I'm going solo” — is fine)

Step 2 · Choose the invitation style (check one)

  • Low-key text: one short message, zero pressure
  • Standing plan: “we already do X together; make this the next X”
  • Fun deadline: bring a snack; swap sprint checkmarks at the end
  • Going solo: skip to the solo reminder below and stop here

Step 3 · The message (copy, tweak, send)

“Hey! I'm doing a one-day writing retreat at home on Saturday — timers, snacks, no evaluating each other. Want to claim a chair? It's 9-ish to 5-ish; you'd need your laptop and a water bottle. No prep needed. Totally fine if it's a no.”

Ultra-short version:

“Saturday writing retreat at my place. Timers + snacks + quiet. You in?”

Deal-breakers rule: add details they'd genuinely need (allergies, stairs, a scent-sensitive pet). Otherwise keep it short — you both hate long invites.

Step 4 · Response plan

They say…You do…
“Yes!”Send the retreat-day schedule, lock the date, done.
“Maybe / that week is bad”Offer one alternate Saturday, then let it go.
“No”“No worries — I'll wave at you from the retreat.” Then don't mention it again.
No replyWait four days, send one friendly reminder, then let it go.
“What should I bring?”“Laptop or notebook, water bottle. I'll handle snacks.”

Step 5 · Day-of logistics

  • Two chairs, two power outlets (or two extension cords).
  • Two of every snack you'd guard with your life.
  • Extra printed schedule + warm-up prompts for them.
  • Extra tent card: “buddy mode” (from the tent-cards sheet).
  • Agreed rule for the day: we don't read each other's work unless it's offered.
Solo reminder: a solo retreat is a complete retreat. This planner is an invitation, never an obligation — the kit works perfectly with a party of one.
