Layout tips

  • Retreat-day schedule prints best in landscape — your browser's print dialog may pick this up automatically; if not, choose Landscape.
  • Tent cards: print the page, cut along the dashed frames, fold on the dashed seams. Pocket-notebook scale also works if you print at 50%.
  • Print double-sided for the goal one-pager and reflection sheet to keep the folder slim.
