# Free-Writing-Retreat-Day Kit: Host Guide

Welcome to your very own at-home Saturday writing retreat. Everything here is
explained like a friend would: plain English, step by step, no pressure. You
will not need to buy anything, sign up for anything, or share any data. You
print a few pages, set up a quiet corner, and write for one lovely day.

**Made by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** - free to use, free to share (MIT license).

## The shape of the day (at a glance)

- **9:00** - Set up, stretch, coffee. Goal-setting one-pager.
- **9:30** - Warm-up prompts (15 minutes, low stakes).
- **9:45** - First writing sprint (25 min) + break.
- Then sprints and breaks alternate all day, with a long lunch and a short
  afternoon snack play (that's what the tent cards are for).
- **4:30** - Final wrap-up sprint.
- **5:00** - Reflection sheet, then you're done. Really done. No homework.

The full timed schedule lives on the schedule printable. Use it as-is or shade
the times to fit your life.

## How to use the writing sprints

1. **Decide the sprint length before you sit down** (25 minutes is the default;
   15 is great if your brain is twitchy).
2. **Pick a target** from your goal one-pager - not "write well," just "words
   on the page."
3. **Start the timer.** The [Write Sprint Timer web
   app](https://github.com/dylanasdale/write-sprint-timer-webapp) works great
   here, and so does any kitchen timer.
4. **When it rings, stop.** Even mid-sentence. Stopping on purpose makes it
   easier to come back.

### Sprint rules that make it easier

- No editing. Editing is a *different* job and this day is for drafting.
- Stuck? Write "stuck, thinking about..." and keep typing. It counts.
- Water every break. Snack when a tent card tells you to.

## The breaks are not optional

Breaks are part of the retreat, not a detour from it. If you are a
low-energy-brained person (hello, fellow ADHD friends), the schedule is built
so the *rest* is pre-planned and you never have to decide whether to keep
going. The decision is made. You rest. Then the next sprint starts fresh.

## Inviting one friend (the soft version)

Retreats are better with one companion, and one is the magic number - no
group logistics, no scheduling nightmare. The invite planner walks you
through: pick the person, pick your message, send it, and handle every
possible response gracefully (including "no" and no reply at all). If inviting
anyone sounds exhausting, skip it - a solo retreat is a complete retreat.

## How to print

- Prefer paper? Open any file from `print/` in a browser and choose
  **Print**. Pages are styled for US Letter (A4 works fine too), with margins
  and page breaks already handled.
- Prefer screens? Every printable exists as plain Markdown in `printables/`.

## No data, ever

This kit collects nothing, tracks nothing, and stores nothing. It is just
files you keep. No accounts, no forms, no "sign up to unlock." Hand it to a
friend and they need nothing but a printer.
