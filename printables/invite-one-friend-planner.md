# Printable: Invite-One-Friend Planner

> One friend. Not five, not a whole Discord. One. This sheet turns "maybe I
> should invite someone" into a sent message within ten minutes.

## Step 1: Pick the person (2 minutes)

Who would make the day *easier*, not more exciting?

**Good picks:** someone who also wants quiet time to make something; the
friend you can sit beside for two hours in silence without it being weird;
someone who'd happily race you for word counts... or nap through a sprint
without a single follow-up question.

**Skip for now:** anyone whose messages during a retreat you'd want to
answer, anyone you'd want to impress, anyone who might ask to see the work.

My pick: ____________________ *(a first name or "I'm going solo" is fine)*

## Step 2: Choose the invitation style (check one)

- [ ] **Low-key text:** one short message, zero pressure
- [ ] **Standing plan:** "we already do x together; make this the next x"
- [ ] **Fun deadline:** bring a snack, swap sprint checkmarks at the end
- [ ] **Going solo:** fill in the solo row of the table below and stop here

## Step 3: The message (copy, tweak, send)

Short version:

> Hey! I'm doing a one-day writing retreat at home on Saturday - timers,
> snacks, zero evaluating each other. Want to claim a chair? It's 9-ish to
> 5-ish, you'd need your laptop and a water bottle. No prep needed. Totally
> fine if it's a no.

Ultra-short version:

> Saturday writing retreat at my place. Timers + snacks + quiet. You in?

Deal-breakers rule: if there's a detail they'd genuinely need (allergies,
scent-sensitive pets, a flight of stairs), add it. Otherwise, keep it short -
you both hate long invites.

## Step 4: Response plan (weird but effective)

| They say... | You do... |
|---|---|
| "Yes!" | Send the retreat-day schedule link, lock the date, done |
| "Maybe / that week's bad" | Offer one alternate Saturday, then let it go |
| "No" | "No worries - I'll wave at you from the retreat." Then don't mention it again |
| No reply | Wait four days, send one friendly reminder, then let it go |
| "What do I need to bring?" | "Laptop or notebook, water bottle. I'll handle snacks." |

## Step 5: Day-of logistics

- [ ] Two chairs, two power outlets (or two extension cords)
- [ ] Two of every snack you'd guard with your life
- [ ] Extra printed schedule + warm-up prompts for them
- [ ] Extra printed schedule (PDF one-pager link) already sent
- [ ] Extra tent card: "buddy mode" (from the tent-cards sheet)
- [ ] Agreed rule for the day: we don't read each other's work unless offered

## Solo reminder

A solo retreat is a complete retreat. This planner is an invitation, never an
obligation - the kit works perfectly with a party of one.
