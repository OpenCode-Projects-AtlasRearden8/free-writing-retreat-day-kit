> Twenty minutes at the end of the retreat. This is how one good Saturday
> turns into a repeatable practice instead of a nice fluke. Answer honestly;
> keep it short; then stop thinking about writing until tomorrow.

## A closing note

Retreats are practice, not judged performances. Whatever today produced -
whole chapters, one good scene, a messy 340 words, or mostly snacks and
notes - you showed up for your writing. That is the actual skill most writers
are missing, and you already have it. See you next Saturday.
