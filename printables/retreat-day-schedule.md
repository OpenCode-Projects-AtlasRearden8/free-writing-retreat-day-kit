# Printable: Retreat-Day Schedule with Sprints and Breaks

> Print me, stick me on the fridge, and stop making decisions all day.
> Times below are a starting point - shift them however your morning goes.

## Saturday Retreat Schedule

| Time | Block | Length | What happens |
|------|-------|--------|--------------|
| 9:00 | Settle in | 15 min | Coffee, water bottle filled, phone in another room, goal-setting one-pager filled out |
| 9:15 | Warm-up | 15 min | One warm-up prompt, written fast and sloppy. No editing allowed |
| 9:30 | Sprint 1 | 25 min | Draft/work. Timer on. Stop when it rings |
| 9:55 | Break | 10 min | Stand, stretch, water (check the hydration tent card!) |
| 10:05 | Sprint 2 | 25 min | Draft/work |
| 10:30 | Break | 15 min | Snack time - snack tent card approved. Maybe a short walk |
| 10:45 | Sprint 3 | 25 min | Draft/work |
| 11:10 | Long break | 20 min | Move your body. Chat with your retreat friend if you invited one |
| 11:30 | Sprint 4 | 25 min | Draft/work - this is usually everyone's best sprint |
| 11:55 | Lunch | 60 min | Real food, off the chair. Nap or walk if you need one |
| 12:55 | Re-settle | 10 min | Water, snack, re-read your goal one-pager |
| 1:05 | Sprint 5 | 25 min | Draft/work. Afternoon brain is slower - that's fine |
| 1:30 | Break | 10 min | Stretch, water, look out a window |
| 1:40 | Sprint 6 | 25 min | Draft/work |
| 2:05 | Break | 20 min | Snack + movement. Sit somewhere different for sprint 7 |
| 2:25 | Sprint 7 | 25 min | Draft/work |
| 2:50 | Break | 10 min | Water, eyes off screen |
| 3:00 | Choose-your-own block | 45 min | A) Two more sprints + a break, B) Quiet reading of your draft, C) Gentle planning for next weekend, D) End early. All valid |
| 3:45 | Break | 15 min | Snack. Last water refill |
| 4:00 | Sprint 8 | 50 min | One long final sprint - or six words and a nap. Both count |
| 4:50 | Wrap-up | 10 min | Save everything. Write tomorrow's first line while it's fresh |
| 5:00 | Reflection | 20 min | End-of-day reflection sheet. Journal lightly. Done at 5:20 |

## Sprint rules

1. Timer decides; you don't.
2. When it rings, stop mid-sentence if you have to. Stopping on purpose makes the next sprint easier.
3. No editing today. Editing gets its own day.
4. Every break includes water. Non-negotiable.
5. If a sprint is going badly, shorten it. A bad 10 minutes is fine; a bad 50 is punishment.

## Scaling options

- **Half-day:** run blocks from 9:00 through lunch only (about 4 sprints).
- **Low-energy day:** 15-minute sprints, 20-minute breaks, skip blocks 6-8.
- **With a friend:** all blocks are the same; you just have someone to wave at across the table.
